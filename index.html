Information about this dashboard

This dashboard brings together statistics on road length, road condition and surface defects instructed and repaired in Northern Ireland. The information is published in an official statistics report produced by the Analysis, Statistics and Research Branch (ASRB) of the Department for Infrastructure (DfI). The full publication is available on the DfI website.
